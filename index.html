04 / The thinking behind the words

how i actually work

My process is
part instinct,
part intention.

a little less blah. a little more aha.

I like copy that feels effortless to read, which usually means being thoughtful before the first word ever hits the page. I start with people, not punchlines.

01

Get curious before getting clever.

Who are we talking to? What do they actually care about? What do they need to understand, feel or do next?

02

Find the one clear thing.

Pull the useful truth out of the noise. A strong message should be easy to repeat and hard to confuse.

03

Write it like someone would say it.

Read it aloud. Lose the filler. Keep the rhythm. Make room for personality without making the reader work for it.

04

Make every word earn its place.

Then edit, test the tone, and ask whether the copy is doing its job, not just sounding nice.

a little logic. a lot of feeling.
